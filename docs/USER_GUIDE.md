# ShuleSoft User Guide (School Admin)

Welcome to ShuleSoft! This guide will help you manage your school efficiently, from onboarding students to generating report cards.

## 1. Getting Started

### Registration
1. Navigate to the **Register** page.
2. Select your preferred subscription plan (Starter, Pro, or Elite).
3. Provide your school's identity and your administrator details.
4. **Important**: Your school email will be your primary Super Admin login.

### Login
- Use your credentials at the **Login** page.
- If you forget your password, contact your platform administrator for a reset.

## 2. Student & Staff Management

### Adding Students
- Navigate to the **Students** tab.
- Click **Add Student**.
- Fill in the Admission Number, Name, Grade, and Stream.
- The system will alert you if you exceed your plan's student limit.

### Managing Teachers
- Use the **Teachers** tab to keep a directory of your academic staff, their subjects, and contact details.

## 3. Academic Records (CBC & Grading)

- **Grading**: Navigate to the **Grading** section to input student marks.
- **CBC Assessments**: Use the specialized CBC interface to track Core Competencies and create detailed student portfolios.
- **Report Cards**: Generate print-ready report cards that align with KNEC standards instantly.

## 4. Fees & Finance

### Tracking Payments
- The **Fees** tab gives you a birds-eye view of your school's financial health.
- **KPIs**: Track Total Collected, Outstanding Balances, and Expected Revenue.

### Recording Payments
1. Search for a student in the Fees table.
2. Click the **Pay** button.
3. Enter the amount and M-PESA transaction code.
4. The system will instantly generate a **Digital Receipt** that you can print or save.

## 5. Subscription & Billing

- Navigate to **Settings > Billing** to see your current plan and expiry date.
- **Renewal**: To renew, follow the M-PESA instructions in the billing dashboard and submit your transaction code.
- **Upgrading**: You can switch plans anytime as your student population grows.

## 6. Multi-Term Support
- Use the **Term Switcher** in the top navigation or sidebar to view historical data or prepare for upcoming terms.
- **Note**: Grading and Fee summaries update automatically based on your selected term.
