# ShuleSoft SaaS Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 20, 2024

ShuleSoft is committed to protecting the privacy and security of your school's data. This policy explains how we collect, use, and protect your information.

## 1. Information We Collect
- **School Identity**: Name, location, and contact information.
- **Administrative Data**: Names and emails of school staff.
- **Student Records**: Names, grades, and academic performance data provided by you.
- **Financial Records**: Fee payment history and M-PESA transaction codes.

## 2. How We Use Information
- To provide school management functionality.
- To process subscriptions and verify payments.
- To provide technical support.
- To improve platform performance and features.

## 3. Data Protection (Security)
- We use industry-standard encryption for data in transit and at rest.
- **Data Isolation**: We use Row-Level Security (RLS) to ensure that your school's data is never visible to other schools.
- **Access Control**: Only authorized administrators can access your school's workspace.

## 4. Data Ownership
- You retain full ownership of all school data uploaded to ShuleSoft.
- You have the right to export your data at any time.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing
- We do **not** sell or share your school's data with third-party advertisers.
- We may share information with necessary service providers (e.g., Supabase, M-PESA) only to the extent required to provide our services.

## 6. Data Retention
- We retain your data as long as your subscription is active.
- Upon termination, you may request the permanent deletion of all school records.

## 7. Contact Us
For questions regarding your data or this policy, contact us at [shulesoft@gmail.com](mailto:shulesoft@gmail.com).
